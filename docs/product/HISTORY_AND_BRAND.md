# Product history and brand record

## Recovered timeline

| Period | Recovered design state |
|---|---|
| November 2025 | Strijder was defined as an all-in-one in-vehicle safety/security dashcam product. The initial static “Strijder Vehicle Safety” site was created around diagnostics, security, evidence, and offline-first operation. |
| November 2025 | Strijder was presented under the then-used Aegorionyx holding-company concept alongside FixIt QC, with the parent positioned around safety, security, and compliance R&D. This is historical corporate context; the repository does not establish current ownership or branding. |
| November 2025 | An early rugged connector panel used DEUTSCH DT/DTM/HDP20, SMA LTE, and four FAKRA camera connections. |
| November 2025 | The design expanded into a larger functional connector family covering power, validation, CAN/OBD/sensing, interlocks, lighting controller/selector, main dashcam head, CAD/Windows display, and auxiliary display. |
| November 2025 | Cadmium proof work used a Windows WPF application with switchable Dispatch Console and in-vehicle modes, shared calls/units/navigation steps, and a WebView2-hosted Mapbox map. |
| Late 2025 | Cadmium UI direction was metallic gold and pearl white, modern and personable rather than chaotic or dated. |
| April 2026 | Strijder Vision was described as a rugged police/public-safety in-vehicle computer for patrol, fire, EMS, and security, combining recording, diagnostics, communications, mapping, tracking, storage, and optional cloud services. |
| May 2026 | The high-processing intelligent dashcam concept was tied to NVIDIA hardware and Linux, operating behind the scenes without a conventional client-facing UI. Cadmium remained the dispatch/CAD system. |
| August 2026 | The product hierarchy was clarified: Cadmium is CAD only and is integrated inside the Strijder Vision Pro EVS dashcam/operator system. Power planning used an approximately 75 W initial load and a larger UPS energy reserve than the ideal 20-minute calculation. |
| September 2026 | The engineering dossier established Vision, Vision Pro EVS, and Cadmium boundaries; added a provisional 22-contact peripheral power allocation and 4.17 A/contact worst-case planning value at 9 V with two parallel feed/return contacts. |

The timeline records design evolution, not commercial launch or production completion.

## Names and boundaries

### Strijder

The master product brand. “Strijder Vehicle Safety” is the name used by the original website.

### Strijder Vision

The complete in-vehicle safety, evidence, diagnostic, communications, and control platform.

### Strijder Vision Pro EVS

The integrated high-compute Emergency Vehicle System configuration with NVIDIA/Linux processing, cameras, operator hardware, vehicle interfaces, lighting controls, and Cadmium integration.

### Strijder Cadmium CAD

The dispatch software only. Its two user surfaces are the Dispatch Console and in-vehicle MDT.

## Existing repository identity

The original site uses:

- title: `Strijder Vehicle Safety`;
- description: “in-vehicle diagnostics, security, and dashcam intelligence”;
- primary colors: deep black, gold, and silver/pearl;
- a gold and white dimensional S emblem;
- language emphasizing offline operation and guardian-like protection.

The site navigation contains Features, Technology, and Contact, with an access-request call to action.

## Later logo hard-lock

A later branding direction imposed these constraints:

- preserve the exact dimensional S/triangular geometry;
- retain a forward tilt;
- use a copper-to-violet liquid-metal/glass sheen;
- use a black background;
- do not introduce a shield;
- do not introduce a sword.

This conflicts with the gold/white repository asset and gold/silver site. The conflict is unresolved. Any redesign must preserve both histories until the product owner explicitly selects the production system.

## UI brand versus corporate mark

The Cadmium interface direction—metallic gold and pearl white—may coexist with a copper-to-violet corporate mark if treated as separate layers:

- product mark and marketing imagery;
- functional application palette;
- operational status colors;
- map day/night styles.

Status colors must never be replaced merely for brand consistency if doing so reduces operational clarity or accessibility.

## Voice and positioning

Recovered positioning themes:

- vigilant rather than militaristic;
- premium and precise rather than gadget-like;
- integrated but modular;
- protective and trustworthy;
- functional without cloud dependence;
- appropriate for fleets and public safety.

Avoid unsupported marketing claims about AI accuracy, evidentiary admissibility, certifications, autonomous safety, or universal vehicle compatibility.

## Commercial status

No final commercial model, SKU matrix, pricing, licensing, warranty, subscription, evidence-storage pricing, installation price, or channel strategy was recovered. The existing site’s “Request Access” framing suggests pre-release or controlled access rather than general availability.
